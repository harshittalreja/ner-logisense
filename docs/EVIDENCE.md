# Real-world evidence used in the presentation

Compiled on 25 Sep 2026 from web research for `presentation/NER-LogiSense_Impact_Deck.pptx`. Each figure lists its source. Before quoting any of them externally, re-open the source. News pages move, and early casualty counts get revised.

Confidence levels:
- **High:** the figure was read on the source page.
- **Medium:** the figure came from a search snippet or a secondary summary.

## Numbers produced by the prototype

All prototype numbers in the deck come from `python -m tools.case_studies`, which writes `analysis/case_studies.json`. They come from a **simulated** world with synthetic hazard labels. Present them as "how the method behaves", not as field measurements.
